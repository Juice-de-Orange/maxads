🔎 What is counted — and what is not

  • Impressions are counted once per visitor, banner and day. A reload does not inflate the number.
  • Sites, on the other hand, are recorded on every request — otherwise every page a returning visitor sees would be missing.
  • No IP addresses. Visitors are told apart by a hash of IP, user agent, the date and a secret. The next day the same visitor can no longer be recognised.
